# CyberDyne Systems - Q2 2026 Internal Memo

## Financial Restructuring
Due to lower than expected Q1 revenues, the CFO has mandated budget cuts across all exploratory R&D projects.

## Project Phoenix Update
Under the new leadership of Miles Dyson Jr., Project Phoenix has been re-evaluated. Despite unexpected breakthroughs in the mesh network protocols, the board has reduced the project's budget by $200,000. The team must now expect a working prototype by November with the reduced capital.

## Q2 Financials
Revenue for Q2 rebounded significantly to $820,000. This was largely driven by the unexpected licensing of our legacy AI algorithms to a major robotics firm in Japan.

## Security Notice
All employees must update their keycards by August 15th, 2026, as we are migrating to a new biometric security system in the main lobby.
